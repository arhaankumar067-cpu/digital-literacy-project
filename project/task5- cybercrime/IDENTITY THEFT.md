## IDENTITY THEFT





Identity theft is a type of cybercrime where someone steals another person’s personal information and uses it without their permission, usually for financial gain.



Ananya, a college student, often sat at her favorite café to study and use free Wi-Fi. One day, she logged into her bank account and social media while connected to the public network. Everything seemed normal at the time. However, a hacker on the same network managed to capture her login details without her knowing.



A few days later, Ananya noticed several messages from her bank about transactions she never made. At the same time, her friends told her they had received strange messages from her social media account asking for money. She soon realised that someone had taken control of her accounts.



In most cases, identity theft happens step by step. First, attackers gather personal information through unsafe networks, fake links, or apps. Then, they use this information to log into accounts. Finally, they misuse the identity for fraud or other illegal activities.



Students are often targeted because they frequently use public Wi-Fi and may not be aware of the risks. The effects can be serious, including loss of money, stress, and damage to one’s reputation.



This situation highlights how important it is to stay careful online, avoid unsecured networks, and never share sensitive information casually.
