DO’s:

Do think before posting anything online.
Do protect your personal information (privacy settings, strong passwords).
Do verify information before sharing to avoid spreading fake news.
Do maintain respectful and positive communication with others.
Do use social media for learning, networking, and skill-building.

DON’Ts:

Don’t share sensitive personal details (address, phone number, passwords).
Don’t engage in cyberbullying, trolling, or hate speech.
Don’t post inappropriate or offensive content.
Don’t believe or spread unverified information.
Don’t spend excessive time on social media affecting studies or health.
