## ONLINE SAFETY CHECKLIST

* Use strong and unique passwords for all accounts; avoid using the same password everywhere.
* Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on email, social media, and banking apps.
* Never share OTPs, PINs, or passwords with anyone, even if they claim to be from the bank.
* Avoid using public Wi-Fi for logging into banking or sensitive accounts.
* Always verify links, emails, and messages before clicking—watch out for fake offers and phishing scams.
* Keep your devices updated with the latest software and antivirus protection.
* UPI Safety: Double-check the receiver’s name before sending money via UPI.
* UPI Safety: Never approve unknown “collect requests” or payment requests on apps like Google Pay/PhonePe.
* Log out from shared or public devices after use.
* Be careful about what you share on social media; limit personal information visibility.
